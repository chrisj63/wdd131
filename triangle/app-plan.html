Triangle Project Plan

Chris Johnson

WDD 131.A4

Angle and side length triangle calculator

Purpose

Assist those learning about triangles to solve the length of all sides and the degrees of all three angles.

Audience

Students of triangles or people struggling to understand how to use triangles.

Dynamic elements

When students input either 2 angles and a side length or 2 side lengths and an angle, then the javascript will decide which formulas are needed to solve the remaining sides and angles. These triangles will then show all 3 angles and the length of all three sides.
